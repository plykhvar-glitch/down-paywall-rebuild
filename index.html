What this is

We read DOWN the way a new single meets it, then rebuilt the screen where VIP is decided.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one app. We went through DOWN’s onboarding and VIP paywall as you show them on downapp.com and in your App Store listing, from GET DOWN or GET DATE to the highlight stickers, and checked every price against your App Store in-app purchases.

It is a clean paywall: the purple-pink glow, four short benefits, big plan cards and one pink button. What it does not do is use what DOWN just learned. Maya said GET DOWN, picked Wicked Smart and is browsing Nearby in Austin. The paywall says “See who liked you!” to everyone, whatever button opened it. Nothing on it says every member is photo-verified, the 12 MONTH card bills every 3 months, and closing it ends the conversation.

We did not redesign your paywall

The glow on navy, the round icon bubbles and your four benefit lines, the plan cards with the pink border and tick, the pink “Save” pill and the pink Continue button all stay. Only the words, the order, one line of proof and one small sheet change.

Paywall rebuild · October 2026

The VIP paywall, finishing what the onboarding started

Same screen, same benefits, same art direction, prices straight from your App Store. Five changes, each fed by something DOWN already asks, already sells or already publishes. Flip Today above the phone to see the version on downapp.com.

1

Their answer in the headline

Today
See who liked you! for everyone, whether they said GET DOWN or GET DATE, and whatever highlight they picked.
Now
Uses: the first name from sign-up, GET DOWN or GET DATE, the highlight sticker, and the city Nearby already uses. Your own words: “who’s DOWN” and “a DATE”.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +15–20%to purchase
2

The feature they tapped, first

Today
The same four lines in the same order, whatever opened VIP, with Date around the world! greyed out.
Now
Uses: which button opened the paywall (Visitors, Flirt, the end of today’s profiles, a new location) and your four benefit lines, word for word.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +10–15%to purchase
3

Your proof, under the button

Today
Nothing between the plans and Continue says who else is here, or that they are real.
Now
★ 4.3 · 130K ratings · Every member photo-verified right under Continue, where people decide.
Uses: 4.3 from 130,361 ratings on your US listing, and “we verify all users from the start” from the same page. Fake profiles are the doubt your one-star reviews raise most.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +10–15%to purchase
4

Prices that add up, per week

Today
12 MONTH at $7.99/mo with 11.99 struck through and Save 30%, then Billed Quarterly $29.99/3mo. The second card, 1 WEEK, says $19.99/wk.
Now
Uses: your US App Store list: Down VIP, 1 Week $12.99 · 1mo VIP $19.99 · 3mo Upgraded Dating & Matches $29.99. $29.99 over 13 weeks is $2.31 a week, 82% less than weekly.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataARPU +8–15%more people on 3 months
5

A smaller yes on close

Today
No second offer: closing the paywall ends it.
Now
Uses: your own add-on Be the Top Profile: Get More Matches & Dates, the 1 Week Profile Boost at $6.99 on the US App Store. No discount on VIP, no countdown.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +3–6%from people who would have closed
